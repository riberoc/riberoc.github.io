Property Redemption Terms

Standard liquidity guidelines for non-traded real estate investment trusts (REITs) and private syndications.

  • 1. Lock-Up Period

    The initial timeframe (typically 12 to 36 months) during which an investor is strictly prohibited from withdrawing or redeeming any capital from the fund.

  • 2. Redemption Windows & Frequency

    Once the lock-up expires, funds generally only process withdrawal requests at specific intervals—most commonly on a quarterly or semi-annual basis.

  • 3. Redemption Penalties (Discounts to NAV)

    Early redemptions are often penalized to discourage short-term trading. For example, withdrawing in Year 2 might yield only 95% of the Net Asset Value (NAV), while Year 4 might yield 100%.

  • 4. Fund-Level Gates (Liquidity Caps)

    To protect the underlying physical real estate assets from forced liquidations, managers typically cap total allowable redemptions at 5% of the fund's total NAV per quarter.
