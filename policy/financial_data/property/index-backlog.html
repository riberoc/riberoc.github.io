Redemption Terms & Thresholds

Please take a moment to review our redemption policy guidelines:

  • Accessing Redemption Terms

To view the terms for a specific listing, post a comment on the property of your interest containing the following policy request ID format:

[REGIONAL_MARKER]##,###.##

Once this policy request ID is posted as a comment on the property page, your threshold terms will be generated and made available to your account.

Submitting Your Policy Request

Now that you have retrieved your Policy Request ID, follow these steps to proceed:

  1. Navigate to your chosen property listing.
  2. Scroll down to the comments section of the posting.
  3. Write a new comment containing your exact Policy Request ID.
