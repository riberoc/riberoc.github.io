Redemption Terms & Thresholds

Please take a moment to review our redemption policy guidelines:

  • Threshold-Based Redemption: Redemption terms are calculated and applied on a per-threshold basis. Your eligible rewards, tiers, and conversion rates vary depending on the specific threshold level associated with your account or chosen property.
  • Retrieving Your Specific Threshold: Your exact redemption threshold details can be unlocked and generated for any listing.

To view the threshold terms for a specific listing, post a comment on the property of your interest containing the following policy request ID format:

[REGIONAL_MARKER]##,###.##

Once this policy request ID is posted as a comment on the property page, your threshold terms will be generated and made available to your account.
