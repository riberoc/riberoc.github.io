Hi there! 

I am Marcelo. I am interested in the intersection of AI and cybersecurity. 

My origin story is not as exciting as others'. When I finished high school, I had no idea what I wanted to do with my life. I still don't really know, but at least I have some ideas. 

Anyway, after high school, I did know that computers were pretty important and that software engineers made a good living. At the time, it sounded like a good enough reason to study Computer Science. I know, it is shallow, but it's the truth. Fast-forward a few years, I was lucky enough to actually like CS, I became captivated by computers' complexity and capabilities. It almost seemed like you do anything with them. 

By the end of my Bachelor's, I was really interested in the cybersecurity world. Back then, I listened a lot to[ Darknet Diaries](https://darknetdiaries.com/episode/) (recommend!), and I thought it was really cool. Bad guys vs. good guys, cops vs. criminals, that type of stuff. I liked it so much that I enrolled in a Master's in Cybersecurity. This time I knew I would like it from the start. During my Master's, I realized that AI is also pretty amazing; I mean, look at what LLMs can do nowadays. "How does that even work?" I wondered. Thus, I decided that I must study it as well; at the end of the day, it allowed computers to do even MORE stuff. My Master's then became a mixture of AI and cybersecurity courses, and gradually I became interested in their intersection.

But! I would be lying if I said that those were my only interests. I like to switch up and try to learn from as many fields as possible.
